Why we're doing this

Vinayaka Chavithi is devotion, family, tradition and community — and every year, thousands of idols dissolve into our lakes and rivers along with the paint and plaster that never break down.

How can we honour tradition — while giving something back to nature?

Less Waste — New Plant Life — Eco-Friendly Celebration

The Concept

Six steps

Every family receives an eco-friendly seeded Ganesha, a flower pot, and a simple instruction card. Here's what happens from there.

01

Receive

Collect your kit — idol, pot, seed and card.

02

Worship

Celebrate exactly as your family always has.

03

Immerse

In a bowl of water at home — the idol softens.

04

Germinate

Plant the softened idol in the pot. Water daily.

05

Grow

A seedling breaks through within days.

06

Replant

Move it to soil — your Ganesha becomes a tree.
